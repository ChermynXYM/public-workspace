# Solution Manual Collection 
**Non-Commercial · Freely Shared · Open to Community Feedback**

This repository contains detailed solutions to selected exercises from classical mathematics textbooks. The project will be expanded to include additional texts over time. All content reflects the author's independent study and is provided solely as a reference for fellow learners. 

---

## Scope and Coverage

The manuals contain solutions to exercises that interested the author during study. Emphasis is placed on core chapters and fundamental concepts, with problems selected to support both foundational understanding and deeper insight. Each solution adheres closely to the logical framework and expository style of its source text.

> **Current Status**:  
> - ✅ *Introduction to Linear Algebra* by Serge Lang (Updates TBD) 

---

## Features

- **Rigorous Derivations**  
  Every step is explicitly justified, avoiding unjustified leaps. Solutions are constructed to align with the axiomatic or conceptual approach of the original work.

- **Clear Typesetting**  
  All content is typeset in LaTeX using standard mathematical packages. Problem statements and solutions are clearly distinguished for ease of reading. 

- **Errata Notes**  
  Minor typographical or notational slips observed during study are documented in Acknowledgment and marked in red within the PDFs.
